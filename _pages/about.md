I am currently a 4th year honours economics student at the UBC Vancouver School of Economics. At UBC, I work as a research assistant for [Dr. Miguel Ortiz](https://miguelortizp.github.io/) on a randomized control trial project in Nigeria. I have also worked with [Dr. JC Punongbayan](https://jcpunongbayan.com/) from the University of the Philippines on various research projects, and am a co-author with [Dr. Jeff Arapoc](https://cem.uplb.edu.ph/personnel/arapoc-jefferson-a/) and [Dr. Nova Nguyen](https://cem.uplb.edu.ph/personnel/nguyen-ma-nova/). I also got the privilege to work at the Department of Economy, Planning, and Development of the Philippines under [ASec. Erick Planta](https://www.oecd-events.org/e/cop28/speaker/6d2f1065-c587-ee11-8923-6045bd8eadf6/roderick-m-planta) and [USec. Joseph Capuno](https://econ.upd.edu.ph/joseph-j-capuno-phd/).

My research interests revolve around development economics, international trade, industrial organization, political economy, and economic history.
